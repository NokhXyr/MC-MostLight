<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/lang-en.gif" alt="English" width="800"></p>
<p><span style="font-size:18px;color:#e5e5e5"><strong>MostLight</strong> adds 71 decorative lamps for ceilings, walls, tables, floors and blocks, each in 16 colours, wired to redstone, switches and LED chains.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:20px;color:#f0a53a"><em>Light every build the way you picture it.</em></span></p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/title-features-en.gif" alt="What It Does" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/colors.gif" alt="Five table lamps on a counter switch between mixed, white, warm and cool colours, each lighting the wall in its colour" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/shaders.gif" alt="The same street at night under Solas, with lamps lighting in their own colours, then under Complementary, with a warm light" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/lamp.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>71 lamps:</strong></span> chandeliers, sconces, street lamps, lava lamps, light blocks, each in 16 colours.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/wrench.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Finishes and tones:</strong></span> 10 frame finishes and 5 light tones, set with the Designer Wrench.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/bolt.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>LED strips and garlands:</strong></span> several per block, joining up at corners, around pillars, in L and T shapes.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/gear.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Animated lamps:</strong></span> spinning gears, flowing lava lamps, ceiling fans that turn.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/puzzle.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Pistons and Create:</strong></span> lamps move with pistons and Create contraptions, keeping their look.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/sun.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Shader friendly:</strong></span> coloured light with Solas, clean warm light with Complementary.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/title-control-en.gif" alt="Light It Up" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/chain.gif" alt="A lever is pulled and the 27 LED strips chained around the ceiling of a room light up at once" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/lamp.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Click:</strong></span> on and off; sneak-click for 4 brightness levels.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/connector.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>LED Connector:</strong></span> chains strips and light blocks: one redstone signal lights up to 256.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/icons/remote.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:18px"><strong>Switches and remote:</strong></span> light switch, dimmer, double switch for fans, up to 64 lamps each.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-MostLight@main/images/bench.gif" alt="The Lamp Workbench catalogue: the Table tab, then the table lamp, the orange colour and the Craft button" width="800"></p>
<p><span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Craft the <strong>Lamp Workbench</strong>: a crafting table, 2 iron ingots, glowstone dust and 2 planks.</p>
<p><span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Pick a lamp in its catalogue and choose a colour; the materials come from your inventory.</p>
<p><span style="color:#f0a53a;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> Place it and click it. JEI is optional and lists every recipe.</p>
</div>
